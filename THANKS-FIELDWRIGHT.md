# Fieldwright attribution

Buildwright thanks Jeffrey Lueken (Venah) and the Fieldwright project for their
contribution to the Vintage Story blueprint and building-tool ecosystem.

The current client adapts parts of Fieldwright's selection, projection,
matching, configuration, BlockEntity mesh reconstruction, and paste workflow.
Those portions remain covered by Fieldwright's MIT License. The complete notice
is preserved in `LICENSE-Fieldwright.txt`.

Buildwright does not bundle or link Fieldwright.dll, Fieldwright binaries,
textures, or other Fieldwright assets. Support for Fieldwright JSON wrapper
fields is format interoperability implemented in Buildwright.

`COPYRIGHT.md` and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` for the boundaries between original
